Drop a folder's .jpg and .xml files here, or use Open files. Photos and labels are paired by name (DJI_0001.JPG with DJI_0001.xml).

Click a stall to cycle unset → occupied → empty. Press ? for keys.

Keys

clickcycle unset → occupied → empty
o / 1stall under the mouse: occupied
e / 0stall under the mouse: empty
u / Backspacestall under the mouse: unset (not scored)
Shift+Eevery unset stall: empty
Shift+Oevery unset stall: occupied
hhide / show outlines
lhide / show stall ids
zfit / 100% zoom
n / pnext / previous photo
Ctrl/Cmd+Ssave this photo's .xml
? / Escshow / close this help

Leave a stall unset when you cannot tell. Saving downloads the .xml under its own name; move it back over the prefilled file. Changes to other photos are kept while the page is open (marked * in the list).
